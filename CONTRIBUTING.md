# Contributing

BRAKE is currently a private pre-launch design project. Small, reviewable changes are
preferred.

Contributions must:

- preserve the independence and risk disclosures;
- avoid price predictions, investment language, artificial engagement, and spam;
- support peaceful and lawful civic participation;
- avoid publishing personal information or identifiable protest imagery without
  consent; and
- keep all mainnet, live-posting, and custody features disabled until formally
  approved.

Before proposing a change, run:

```sh
pnpm check
```

Never commit secrets, private keys, seed phrases, access tokens, unpublished wallet
information, or applicant personal data.
